03 — How I Work

Simple process, serious craft.

/01 Brief & Discover We start with a conversation. You share the goal, the audience, and the tone. I ask questions, review references, and map out the direction before touching anything.
/02 Draft & Refine First draft fast, second draft sharp. I build the structure, fill in the substance, then cut what doesn't earn its place. You get a review round with clear tracked changes.
/03 Polish & Deliver Final pass for flow, grammar, and formatting. You receive the finished piece ready to publish — plus any source files or variations you need.
