# How to edit your website

Your website (shoyaibraby.com) gets all its words, pictures and videos from an editing screen called **Sanity Studio**. You change things there, press **Publish**, and the website updates itself about 1–2 minutes later. You can't break the design from there.

**Editing screen:** https://shoyaib.sanity.studio. Sign in with the account you were invited with.

## The menu on the left

| Item | What it changes |
|---|---|
| **Home page** | Everything on the front page, in tabs: Top · About/Services/Process · Work/Writing/Contact · Search & sharing |
| **Writing page** | The heading on the Writing page and the order of your articles |
| **Editing page** | The heading and the two cards on the Editing page |
| **Video editing page / Static design page** | The heading and order of samples on each gallery |
| **Articles** | Your articles. Add, edit or delete them here |
| **Work samples** | Your videos and images. Add, edit or delete them here |
| **Site settings** | Your name, email, Upwork link and the picture shown when someone shares your link |

## Everyday tasks

**Change some text:** open the page → click the field → type → press **Publish** (bottom right). Wait 1–2 minutes and refresh the website.

**Orange or italic words in a heading:** put stars around them, like `Words that *move*`. Press Enter in a heading to start a new line.

**Add an article:** Articles → the **+** (Create) button → fill in Title, click **Generate** next to "Web address", choose a Category and Date, write the text → **Publish**. It appears on the Writing page automatically. In the text box, use the "Paragraph" menu to make a Heading, the list buttons for bullet points, and the **B** / *I* buttons for bold and italic.

**Add a video or design:** Work samples → **+** → pick Video or Image → upload the file → fill in Title, the description and a Tag → **Publish**. It appears at the end of its gallery automatically. To change its position, open "Video editing page" (or "Static design page") and drag it. To show it on the home page too, open Home page → Work tab → Selected work → **Add item**.

**Change the numbers on the home page** ($2K+, 14 projects …): Home page → About/Services/Process tab → About → Numbers.

**Reorder things:** drag the ⋮⋮ handle on the left of any item in a list.

**Undo a mistake:** open the document → the clock icon (History) at the top right → pick an earlier version → **Restore**. Before you publish, **Discard changes** throws away what you typed.

## Good to know

- **Nothing goes live until you press Publish.** Until then your changes are a private draft.
- **Videos:** keep each under about 15 MB (export at 1080p, a short clip). Big files make the site slow on phones.
- **Images:** upload the original; the website resizes them automatically.
- **Image description:** one short sentence saying what's in the picture. It helps blind visitors and Google.
- **Don't change an article's "Web address"** after it's published; links people already shared would stop working.
- If the website hasn't updated after 5 minutes, tell Tabib.
